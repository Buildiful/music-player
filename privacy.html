Privacy

Updated November 18, 2026

Your music stays on your device

Music Player runs in your browser. By default, new imports store a catalog with titles, artists, albums and, where supported, file handles in IndexedDB; they do not copy audio into browser storage. In browsers that cannot retain original file access, source files may need to be selected again after reopening. Song information you edit is saved to the browser catalog without editing the original files. Files are read locally and never uploaded to Buildiful or GitHub Pages.

You can optionally save selected original audio as offline copies, including for use in an iPhone Home Screen installation. This copies audio into IndexedDB and consumes device browser storage. These copies, playlists and edited song details may be deleted when website/app data is cleared or evicted by the browser or operating system. Keep your original files somewhere safe. Audio copied in older versions may also remain until those tracks are relinked or removed. Removing music from this app never removes or changes the original files.

Optional Google Analytics

Only if you choose Allow, we load Google Analytics 4 to measure page visits and basic feature use such as adding songs, editing local catalog data, saving or removing offline copies, managing playlists, or clicking installation/download controls. Google may process cookies, approximate location, browser/device information and interaction data. Our tag disables Google signals and advertising personalization.

We never intentionally transmit song contents, files, paths, filenames, song titles, artist names, edited metadata, playlist names, or playback history to Google Analytics. Events include only restricted action names, category labels and numeric counts. Page paths exclude query parameters and fragments.

Your choice

Before consent, no Google Analytics script is loaded. Declining prevents optional GA collection. Your choice and timestamp are saved in local browser storage, which may be shared by other tools hosted on the same domain using the same module. Use Music Player's “Analytics settings” control to change your choice. Withdrawing consent reloads the app to stop future collection; data already recorded is not automatically erased from Google's systems. Analytics is disabled in a downloaded file:// edition.

File access, offline use and rights

Browsers may ask for file access again when Music Player reopens. Offline availability of the app shell requires its service worker cache; offline playback depends on retained browser copies or accessible source files. Safari and installed Home Screen apps on iPhone cannot generally retain reusable source file handles across sessions. Browser storage is not a backup. The app offers no accounts, advertisements, music uploads, or subscriptions. Please play only files you have permission to use.

See Google's Privacy Policy. For questions, use the contact options on Buildiful's website.
