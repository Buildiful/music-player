Privacy

Updated November 17, 2026

Your music stays with you

Music Player runs in your browser. When you add music, the app stores copies in your device's local browser database so your library and playlists can work offline. Your original files remain where you saved them. We do not upload, access remotely, or collect the audio files or playlists. You can remove songs or clear the entire library in Settings. Clearing the app's browser storage may also erase those local copies and playlists.

Optional Google Analytics

If you choose Allow, we load Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and basic feature use (such as adding files, managing playlists, or clicking install or offline-download controls). This may involve cookies, approximate location, browser/device information, and interaction data processed by Google. We configure Google signals and advertising personalization to be off.

We never intentionally send audio, filenames, song titles, artist names, playlist names, audio contents, or listening history to Google Analytics. Only a limited set of action categories and numeric counts is sent. Page paths exclude query parameters and fragments.

Your choice

Before consent, no Google Analytics tag is loaded. Declining prevents optional analytics from loading. Your choice and its timestamp are saved in local browser storage, potentially shared by tools on the same website origin using the same consent module. Use “Analytics settings” in Music Player to change your choice. Withdrawing consent reloads the app to stop new analytics collection; data collected before withdrawal is not automatically erased from Google's systems. Analytics is disabled when you open a downloaded local-file copy.

Other notes

The app itself has no accounts, ads, subscriptions, or audio uploads. Installing it creates a browser-managed local app; offline availability depends on the browser retaining its app cache and local storage. Playing music does not grant rights to the audio: please use files you have permission to play.

See Google's Privacy Policy. For questions, use the contact options on Buildiful's website.
